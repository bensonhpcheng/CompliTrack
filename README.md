# CompliTrack

A browser-based application designed to streamline audit preparation for Quality Assurance (QA) departments in food and drug manufacturing. CompliTrack focuses on FDA and other applicable regulatory compliance, centralizing document management, providing accountability, and ensuring efficient tracking of supplier/third-party compliance.

## Features

### User Authentication & Authorization

- Secure login and registration with JWT authentication.
- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) with Admin, Manager, and Employee roles.

### Centralized Document Management

- Upload, categorize, and manage supplier documents.
- Extensive metadata fields for comprehensive document tracking.

### Supplier Management

- Maintain supplier profiles with contact information and ingredients supplied.
- Associate documents with specific suppliers for easy retrieval.
